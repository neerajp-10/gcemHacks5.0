Demonstration Advisory Protocol

1. What did we notice? (ಗಮನಿಸಿದ್ದು)

Canopy moisture index reflectance anomaly in North-East quadrant. Vegetative baseline index: 0.58 vs 0.70 benchmark.

2. What could it mean? (ಅರ್ಥವೇನು)

Topsoil drying due to sunny 31°C afternoon exposure and lack of surface mulch, or potential root aeration blockage.

3. What should I do next? (ಮುಂದೇನು)
  • Walk to the North-East boundary and press soil by hand to check moisture depth.
  • Inspect crop stems for yellowing or fungal lesions.
  • If top 5 cm is dry, irrigate in late afternoon when evaporation drops.

How EARTHWORM Works

1. Satellites scan crop light reflectance Sentinel-2 scans districts regularly using multi-spectral bands to measure chlorophyll and green biomass reflection.
2. Local Weather Integration Combines rain forecast and humidity data to estimate if soil drying or crop stress is occurring.
3. Timely Farmer Notice Alerts you before crop stress visibly expands across the field so you save water, inputs, and protect yield.
