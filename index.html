How EARTHWORM Works

1. Satellites scan crop light reflectance Sentinel-2 scans districts regularly using multi-spectral bands to measure chlorophyll and green biomass reflection.
2. Local Weather Integration Combines rain forecast and humidity data to estimate if soil drying or crop stress is occurring.
3. Timely Farmer Notice Alerts you before crop stress visibly expands across the field so you save water, inputs, and protect yield.
